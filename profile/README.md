# ✨ Vibed.win

> A chill corner for vibe coders. Free. Chill. No algorithms. Just coders who get it.

Welcome to the **Vibed.win** GitHub organisation! 

We're a community of developers who code by feel, sharing projects, debugging together, and keeping it chill.

---

## 🌊 What We're About

This is a space for **vibe coders** — people who build for the love of building, not for performance reviews or follower counts. Whether you're coding at 1am or just getting started, you belong here.

---

## 🧩 What We Build

### 🎮 Games & Interactive Projects
From Pong clones with neon aesthetics to original creations — we love making things that are fun to play and build.

### 🤖 Discord Bots
We build tools that bring communities together, like our 24/7 Discord music bots that play audio files as a continuous radio station.

### 🛠️ Developer Tooling
Snippets, starter kits, boilerplates, and cheatsheets — all shared by the community for the community.

### 🎵 Music & Vibes
Code along to 24/7 music streams.
